Peter verified 33 CFR 159: *no US vessel of any size* is required to install a toilet, and MSD rules attach only once one is installed. Leaving an error he has already flagged in your documents is expensive. Correct it everywhere and use the accurate version — it is actually *better* for you, since it widens the population of boats with no facility.

Stop leading with it. Lead instead with what your buyer work actually found: qualified sites already spend $3,000+/month on sanitation. Both may be true, but Peter has the first document and will notice. Add one reconciling line to Module 3 and retire the synopsis from the data room.
