## 1. The band table problem, and why it matters

The repo holds two incompatible specifications. The instructor uses the stricter one.

| | `score-opportunity.md` | `references/studio-os-frameworks.md` |
|---|---|---|
| Market Size S(1) | under $100M | $50M to $250M |
| Market Size M(2) | $100M to $500M | $250M to $1B |
| Market Size L(3) | $500M to $1B | $1B to $10B |
| Resources M(5) | not stated | $250K to $1M |
| Resources L(3) | not stated | $1M to $3M |

Two consequences shaped everything in this plan.

**The platform TAM of $249M sat one million dollars below the M threshold.** Under the instructor's table that scored a 1. Tuning assumptions upward to clear $250M was never an option, since the sensitivity register already placed $249M at the optimistic end. The only honest route past it was a second line, which is what happened.

**The $290,000 raise sits inside the M band of $250K to $1M.** That alone moved Resources Required from 3 to 5.

Quote 34.5, not 38.5. The command file figure will not match what the instructor calculates.
